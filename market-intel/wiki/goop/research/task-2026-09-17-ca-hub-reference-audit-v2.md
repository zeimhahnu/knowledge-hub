## Priority decision: special cash dividend

The evidence supports separating **economic description** from **ISO processing**:

- A special cash distribution is not automatically a `DVOP` Dividend Option. ISO defines `DVOP` around a holder choice between cash and securities.
- If the event is cash-only, the current `DVOP` mapping and voluntary badge are not supported by the ISO definition. The likely direction is the ordinary cash-dividend code family plus a mandatory processing indicator, but the exact market-practice combination should be confirmed before changing production data.
- Vendor “special” treatment is independent of the ISO code: MSCI uses a 5% threshold; FTSE and S&P use classification/pattern rules; STOXX applies a distinct special-cash treatment to Price, Gross Return, and Net Return; VettaFi applies special dividends to all three return variants in its policy. Therefore the event can be “special” for index methodology while still being mandatory for the holder.

## Three fixes first

1. **Correct the ISO/MT564 layer first.** The current code map contains multiple scope errors (`DVOP`, `RHDI`, `SPIN`, `SPLT`, `OFFO`, `REDU`, `DELI`) and the sample is not parseable MT564. This is the highest operational risk because it can make a valid feed look like a different event.
2. **Resolve special cash dividend semantics before changing its badge/code.** The `DVOP` + voluntary combination is the exact kind of plausible shortcut that can misroute mandatory cash distributions. Confirm the local market-practice combination, then update the event taxonomy and rules together.
3. **Replace stale extraction/glossary claims with current vendor distinctions.** In particular, remove MSCI’s blanket TR exclusion, correct STOXX’s special-dividend and HDRI descriptions, and replace Solactive/VettaFi `NULL` claims. These statements drive practitioner interpretation even when the underlying rules are not edited.
